Grade 4 · 4.NF.A.1 · Version 2 (you edited it)

Same bar, cut two ways

Students get: this worked example, then matching (4 pairs).

Warm-up

Show one bar split in half. Ask: if we cut each half again, did the shaded amount change?

Worked example You edited this

Two bars of the same length. The first shows 1/2. The second is cut into fourths, and two fourths are shaded. Line them up: the shaded parts end at the same place.

Check for understanding

Is 3/6 the same amount as 1/2? Use a bar to show why.

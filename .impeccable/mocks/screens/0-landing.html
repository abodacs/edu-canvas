One loop, and the teacher decides at each turn

  1. Teacher

    Write the objective

    Plain words, a grade, and an optional source file.

  2. Edu-Canvas

    Draft and check

    Four variants, checked for structure, curriculum fit and safety before you see them.

  3. Teacher

    Review and approve

    Edit one section at a time. Approved versions never change.

  4. Student

    Practice, then the next step

    One focused activity, graded on the server, with a reason for what comes next.

Grade 4 mathematics — equivalent fractions, source notes for the week.

Prior skills students already have:
- Identify the numerator and denominator of a fraction.
- Compare unit fractions with the same denominator.
- Partition rectangles and circles into equal parts.

New learning for this week:
A fraction can name the same amount in more than one way. One half, two
quarters, and four eighths all cover the same part of the same whole.

Method we use in class:
1. Fold a paper strip into two equal parts and shade one part. Write 1/2.
2. Fold the same strip into four equal parts and shade two parts. Write 2/4.
3. Line the strips up. The shaded lengths match, so 1/2 = 2/4.

Language that must match the source:
- "same whole" — both fractions must describe the same size whole.
- "equal parts" — parts must be equal before we compare them.
- "equivalent" — different names, same amount.

Common mistakes to plan feedback for:
- Adding to numerator and denominator without thinking about the whole.
- Saying 2/4 is bigger than 1/2 because 2 and 4 are bigger numbers.
- Comparing fractions of different wholes.

Vocabulary: equivalent, numerator, denominator, whole, partition, strip.

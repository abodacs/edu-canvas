## Cross-pack blind spots

| Deliberate design choice | What it cannot establish | Evidence still needed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| US Common Core, mostly public schools, ten core Grade 4 teachers | Generalization to other countries, curricula, subjects, grades, school governance or private/charter core users | Confirm the real study segment, curriculum and current classroom task before recruitment |
| Age bands, gender, ethnicity, migration and caregiving vary | Effects of identity, representativeness, intersectional experience or relative prevalence | Voluntary context from real teachers; no skills, attitudes or behavior inferred from demographics |
| Structured degrees, credentials, experience and mathematics-review assumptions | Actual qualifications, content knowledge or error-detection proficiency | Ask about preparation and observe unhinted review; neither credentials nor years certify correctness |
| Most roles can prepare on laptops and read English; varied resources remain authored | Mobile-only authoring, no device, low digital literacy, language processing barriers or actual resource availability | Context screening, accessible alternatives and direct observation on the person's available device |
| Core tool groups are three neither, three LLM, three hypothetical Jev, one both | Tool superiority, adoption rates or causal tool-group differences | Establish actual tools, access, proficiency and policy; use a separately designed fair comparison if warranted |
| Hypothetical Jev use and pinned design confidence rules | Teacher adoption, provider access, accuracy or calibration | Provider evaluation and real workflow evidence; confidence never establishes lesson correctness |
| Neutral prompts precede separate stress blocks | Natural error frequency or an unbiased estimate of review accuracy after exposure to S1 | Record order, prior hints and assistance; keep neutral observations and injected conditions separate |
| Four proposed contact windows for core teachers | Retention, willingness to pay or a completed two-week study | Authorized real sessions, eligible preparation opportunities, actual later choices and separate purchase-process evidence |
| Caregiving and workload described qualitatively | Exact availability, effort, stress or a causal reason for non-use | Ask what planning time existed on the relevant occasion; do not fill gaps with invented minutes |
| Separate screen-reader probe planned | Screen-reader compliance or coverage of all disability needs | Direct checks with suitable assistive technology and real access requirements |
| Synthetic response examples and proposed teacher control | Learner understanding, learning transfer, retention, classroom delivery or safe real-data operation | Separate authorized teacher/learner evidence and required safety gates; this pack contains none |

## What to resolve before a real study

Confirm the segment and school permission route, actual preparation opportunities, approved tool access, accessible tasks, comparable baseline materials, the deployed capability revision and the first unavailable step. Ask who recommends, who approves, who pays and who provides support. **Purchasing willingness remains unknown for all twelve roles.**

Keep [#118](https://github.com/abodacs/edu-canvas/issues/118) and [V12](../../hackathon/meta-global-2026/validation-tracker.md) pending until their real evidence exists. The gaps above are study questions, not findings or a ranked feature backlog.

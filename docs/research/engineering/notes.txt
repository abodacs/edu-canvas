 Where CI time actually goes (and the fix for each):

1. Compute: the job itself. Faster machines help here.

2. Waiting: queues from concurrency caps. A faster machine doesn't shorten a queue.

3. Setup: checkout, installs, cache restores. Shallow checkouts and warm caches.

4. Redundant work: runs for commits you already replaced, the full suite for a docs change. cancel-in-progress, path filters, affected tests only.

5. Idle time: fixed sleeps, healthchecks that wait on an open port instead of a ready database.

Most teams only fix #1. The bigger wins are usually in 2–5, and they live in your workflow files, not your hardware.
